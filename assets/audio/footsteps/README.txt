Footstep recordings go here (.ogg or .wav). They are picked up automatically by file name:
  "snow"            -> walking on snow (also used in the trench if nothing else is there)
  "trench"/"gravel" -> the trampled trench floor
  "wood"            -> the bunker floor and the Maxim platform
Suggested: Kenney "Impact Sounds" (CC0, free, no account): https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
  footstep_snow_000.ogg ... footstep_snow_004.ogg, footstep_wood_000.ogg ... footstep_wood_004.ogg
No files here = no footsteps.
